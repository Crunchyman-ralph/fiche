# The product is named Fiche

After grilling 60+ candidates across French, Italian, Spanish, Latin, Greek, and invented-Romance roots, the product is named **Fiche** (pronounced *feesh*). The name is from the French *fiche* — a focused, structured note or record. Every Doc in Fiche is, literally, a fiche.

## Why

- **The meaning is exact.** A *fiche* in French is a single, structured document about one focused subject — a *fiche produit* (product sheet), *fiche de lecture* (book report), *fiche de poste* (job description). A PRD or brief *is* a fiche on a feature.
- **Common-word familiarity for French speakers, no sophistication tax.** It's everyday French — students, professionals, retirees all use it. Not an obscure literary reference.
- **English speakers will associate it with "microfiche"** — the original compact, structured document storage technology. That association is *fine*: we are the modern, AI-native, multi-player cousin of that idea.
- **Short.** Single syllable, five letters. Lives in the Notion / Linear / Figma length zone.
- **Distinct in tech.** No major tech product owns "Fiche" today.
- **The `.md` TLD is the natural canonical home.** *fiche.md* reads as both "fiche on Moldova's ccTLD" (which the .md hosting market has quietly captured for Markdown tools) and as "fiche, the markdown product." Brand promise is in the URL itself.
- **The bac memory.** The colored draft cards French students use during exams are called *fiches*. A Fiche Doc is the digital, multi-player, AI-native version of that exact artifact.

## Pronunciation note

*Feesh* — one syllable. Not "fitch," not "fee-shay." Some English speakers will mispronounce it the first few times; that becomes a brand quirk (à la *Vite* → "vite" vs "veet") rather than a problem.

## Acknowledged concerns

- **French slang adjacency.** *Fichu* (fee-shoo) means "ruined / screwed" in French slang. Context makes the product name read separately, but worth knowing.
- **Microfiche association** may date the product for some users; we lean into it rather than fight it.
- **Domain stack.** For MVP we use `fiche.ralph.codes` as a no-cost subdomain. `fiche.md` (~$50/yr) is acquired once traction justifies it.

## Considered and rejected (selected highlights from ~60 candidates)

- **Margin** — the original working name. Too generic, heavily collided in search, finance-loaded.
- **Bref** — perfect meaning (French for "brief") but iconic French TV show. Hard veto.
- **Galley / Gloze / Postil** — printer-and-marginalia metaphors. Too obscure; required sophistication to land.
- **Brouillon / Brouyon / Borrador / Bozza** — all mean "draft." Strong meaning, but two-syllable / longer / Italian-or-Spanish-flavored variants felt less ownable than the focused-note framing of *fiche*.
- **Stoa / Tessera / Stanza** — gathering / mosaic / room metaphors from Greek and Latin. Beautiful but more abstract than the concrete "this is a fiche" naming.
- **Marj / Avek / Plumi** — French respells. Lost momentum once *fiche* surfaced as the more grounded choice.
